**Question:** Prolactinomas are the most common type of which category of tumor? [S1][S2][S6]  
**A)** Medullary thyroid carcinoma  
**B)** Pituitary neuroendocrine tumor (PitNET)  
**C)** Ovarian teratoma  
**D)** Adrenal pheochromocytoma  
**Correct Answer:** B [S1][S2][S6]  
**Rationale:** Prolactinomas represent approximately half of all pituitary neuroendocrine tumors and are the most common functioning pituitary adenoma. [S1][S2][S6]
