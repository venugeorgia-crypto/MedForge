**Educational Study Pack: Prolactinoma – Diagnosis and Management**

This study pack provides a concise, evidence-based overview of prolactinoma, a common pituitary neuroendocrine tumor. It is designed for medical students, residents, and healthcare professionals seeking foundational knowledge in endocrinology and pituitary disorders.

The pack includes:
- A summary of clinical presentation in women and men, including symptoms such as oligo/amenorrhea, galactorrhea, and loss of libido [S5].
- An explanation of differential diagnoses, including drug-induced hyperprolactinemia, hypothyroidism, and pituitary stalk compression [S1].
- A review of treatment options, with dopamine agonists (e.g., cabergoline) as the first-line therapy, achieving prolactin normalization in up to 85% of patients and tumor shrinkage in up to 80% [S2].
- Information on surgical and radiation therapy, including transsphenoidal surgery for small tumors or cases of dopamine agonist intolerance, and radiotherapy for refractory cases [S6].
- Considerations for management across the female life cycle, including treatment adjustments during pregnancy and postmenopause, with stable tumor behavior observed after menopause [S4].
- Notes on pediatric cases, emphasizing the need for multidisciplinary care due to differences in presentation and etiology [S5].
