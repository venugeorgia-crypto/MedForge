---

**1. What is the location of the pituitary gland?**  
The pituitary gland is located in the bony sella turcica at the base of the brain. [S2]

---

**2. Name the two main anatomical regions of the pituitary gland.**  
The anterior lobe (adenohypophysis) and the posterior lobe (neurohypophysis). [S2]

---

**3. What is the role of the hypothalamus in relation to the pituitary gland?**  
The hypothalamus regulates the secretion of pituitary hormones. [S2]

---

**4. What are the five hormones secreted by the anterior pituitary?**  
The anterior pituitary secretes five endocrine hormones from five different types of epithelial endocrine cells. (Specific names not provided in evidence.) [S2]

---

**5. What is the function of the pituitary gland in the body?**  
The pituitary is the "master control gland" that produces hormones affecting growth and the functions of other glands. [S3]

---

**6. What are the common symptoms of pituitary tumors?**  
Symptoms include headaches, vision problems, nausea and vomiting, and hormonal imbalances. [S3]

---

**7. What endocrine diseases can result from pituitary tumor-induced hormone overproduction?**  
Pituitary tumors can cause hyperprolactinemia, acromegaly, and Cushing’s disease. [S4]

---

**8. What is the most common treatment for acromegaly and Cushing’s disease?**  
Transsphenoidal surgery is the treatment of choice. [S4]

---

**9. What imaging test is used to diagnose pituitary tumors?**  
Brain MRI is a key diagnostic imaging procedure. [S3]

---

**10. What is the significance of arterial supply to the pituitary gland?**  
Knowledge of arterial supply is important for clinicians and surgeons, particularly in surgical planning and understanding vascular anatomy. [S1]

---

**11. How does dynamic CT help in diagnosing pituitary tumors?**  
Dynamic CT can detect enhancement patterns in the pituitary gland, allowing early identification of microadenomas or small macroadenomas before they become large. [S5]

---

**12. What is the typical enhancement pattern observed in the canine pituitary gland after contrast administration?**  
The pituitary gland shows a bi-exponential enhancement pattern with a slower phase having a half-time of 16.5 minutes. [S5]

---

**13. Why is the size and hormonal activity of a pituitary adenoma clinically relevant?**  
The clinical relevance depends on size, location, hormonal hyper- or hyposecretion, and visual field loss. [S4]

---

**14. What are the treatment options for pituitary tumors besides surgery?**  
Other options include radiation therapy, chemotherapy, and modern medications. [S3][S4]

---

**15. What is the prevalence of pituitary gland tumors in the population?**  
Pituitary gland tumors have a prevalence of approximately 75.67 per 100,000 people. [S4]
