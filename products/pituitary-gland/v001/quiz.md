**MCQ 1**  
**Question:** Which blood vessel primarily supplies the anterior pituitary gland?  
**A)** Internal carotid artery  
**B)** Posterior communicating artery  
**C)** Superior sagittal sinus  
**D)** Ophthalmic artery  
**Correct Answer:** B  
**Rationale:** The posterior communicating artery is a key branch of the internal carotid artery that supplies the anterior pituitary gland. [S1]

**MCQ 2**  
**Question:** The pituitary gland is anatomically connected to which brain structure?  
**A)** Cerebellum  
**B)** Hypothalamus  
**C)** Thalamus  
**D)** Medulla oblongata  
**Correct Answer:** B  
**Rationale:** The pituitary gland is attached to the base of the brain and has a unique connection with the hypothalamus. [S2]
