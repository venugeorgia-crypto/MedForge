**Educational Study Pack: Pituitary Gland Anatomy and Clinical Management**

This study pack provides concise, evidence-based educational content on the anatomy, function, and clinical management of the pituitary gland. Designed for medical students and healthcare learners, it covers key anatomical features, endocrine functions, and common clinical conditions such as pituitary tumors.

**What’s Included:**
- Overview of pituitary gland structure: anterior (adenohypophysis) and posterior (neurohypophysis) lobes, with functional roles and hypothalamic regulation [S2].
- Arterial blood supply: a comprehensive review of the vascular anatomy of the pituitary gland, relevant for surgical and diagnostic planning [S1].
- Clinical presentation of pituitary tumors: common symptoms (headaches, vision problems, nausea, hormonal imbalances), including endocrine disorders such as Cushing’s syndrome and hyperprolactinemia [S3][S4].
- Diagnostic and treatment approaches: imaging (e.g., MRI), hormonal testing, and therapeutic options including transsphenoidal surgery, radiation, and medications [S4].
- Evidence on tumor prevalence: pituitary adenomas occur at a rate of approximately 75–78 per 100,000 individuals, with clinical significance based on size, hormone secretion, and visual field loss [S4].

**Who It Is For:**
Medical students, residents, and healthcare professionals seeking foundational knowledge of pituitary gland physiology and pathology.
