**Question:** Which comorbidity is commonly seen in patients with acromegaly and is driven by growth hormone-induced insulin resistance? [S3]

**B)** Diabetes mellitus  
**C)** Hyperuricemia  
**D)** Hyperlipidemia  
**Correct Answer:** B) [S3]  
**Rationale:** Diabetes mellitus occurs in approximately one-third of acromegaly patients due to growth hormone-induced attenuation of insulin signaling. [S3]

**Question:** What is a unique metabolic feature of diabetes mellitus in acromegaly? [S3]  
**A)** Obesity is always present  
**B)** Insulin resistance occurs independently of obesity  
**C)** Glucose uptake increases in adipose tissue  
**D)** Insulin resistance is due to diet  
**Correct Answer:** B) [S3]  
**Rationale:** Insulin resistance in acromegaly occurs independently of obesity and is associated with a lean phenotype and reduced body adipose tissue mass. [S3]

**Question:** What is the relationship between growth hormone and insulin signaling in acromegaly? [S3]
