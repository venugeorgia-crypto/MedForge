This educational study pack provides a concise, evidence-based overview of acromegaly, designed for medical students and healthcare professionals seeking foundational knowledge.  

The pack includes:  
- A definition of acromegaly and its pathophysiology, including hypersecretion of growth hormone and insulin-like growth factor 1 [S1].  
- Clinical manifestations and common comorbidities, such as cardiovascular, metabolic, and musculoskeletal complications [S4].  
- Diagnostic criteria, including elevated serum IGF-1 and lack of growth hormone suppression after glucose administration, with emphasis on pituitary magnetic resonance imaging [S4].  
- First-line treatment options, such as transsphenoidal pituitary surgery, and alternative medical therapies including somatostatin receptor ligands, cabergoline, and pegvisomant [S4].  
- The association between acromegaly and diabetes mellitus, which occurs in approximately one-third of patients due to growth hormone-induced insulin resistance, even in the absence of obesity [S3].  
- Evidence of increased cardiovascular morbidity and mortality in patients with acromegaly, particularly when diabetes is present [S3].  
- A note on the rising incidence in elderly patients, likely due to increased life expectancy, and the challenges of diagnosis in this group [S5].  
- A discussion of the potential link between long-term growth hormone exposure and tumor development, though current evidence remains debated [S6].
