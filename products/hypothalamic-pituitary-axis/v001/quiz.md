**Question:** Which hormone is released from hypothalamic neurons to initiate ovulation via the hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian (HPO) axis? [S1]  
**A)** Follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)  
**B)** Luteinizing hormone (LH)  
**C)** Gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH)  
**D)** Prolactin  
**Correct Answer:** C [S1]  
**Rationale:** GnRH is released from hypothalamic neurons and triggers the pituitary to secrete LH and FSH, which are essential for ovulation [S1]

**Question:** Leptin influences GnRH neuronal activity through which hypothalamic neurons? [S1]  
**A)** Dopamine and serotonin neurons  
**B)** Proopiomelanocortin (POMC), neuropeptide Y (NPY)/agouti-related peptide (AgRP), and neuronal nitric oxide (NO) synthase (nNOS) neurons  
**C)** Thyrotropin-releasing hormone (TRH) neurons  
**D)** Vasopressin and oxytocin neurons  
**Correct Answer:** B [S1]  
**Rationale:** Leptin acts indirectly on GnRH neurons via adjacent leptin receptor-expressing neurons including POMC, NPY/AgRP, and nNOS neurons [S1]

**Question:** Hypothalamic inflammation can affect ovulation independently of obesity. What does this imply about the hypothalamic-pituitary-ovarian (HPO) axis? [S1]  
**A)** It is only disrupted in obese women  
**B)** It is unaffected by metabolic or inflammatory signals  
**C)** It can be disturbed by non-obesity-related hypothalamic inflammation  
**D)** It is entirely dependent on body weight regulation  
**Correct Answer:** C [S1]  
**Rationale:** Hypothalamic inflammation can disrupt the HPO axis even in the absence of obesity [S1]

**Question:** The World Health Organization classifies ovulation disorders into how many groups based on HPO axis dysfunction? [S2]  
**A)** Two  
**B)** Three  
**C)** Four  
**D)** Five  
**Correct Answer:** B [S2]  
**Rationale:** WHO classifies HPO axis disorders into three groups: hypogonadotropic, eugonadal, and hypergonadotropic hypogonadism [S2]

**Question:** Group I ovulation disorders are characterized by what type of HPO axis dysfunction? [S2]  
**A)** Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism  
**B)** Eugonadal endocrinopathy  
**C)** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism  
**D)** Elevated estradiol levels  
**Correct Answer:** C [S2]  
**Rationale:** Group I disorders involve hypothalamic failure leading to hypogonadotropic hypogonadism [S2]

**Question:** Group II ovulation disorders are typically associated with what physiological state? [S2]  
**A)** Hypogonadotropic hypogonadism  
**B)** Hypergonadotropic hypogonadism  
**C)** Eugonadal state with a wide range of endocrinopathies  
**D)** Absent pituitary hormone secretion  
**Correct Answer:** C [S2]  
**Rationale:** Group II disorders display a eugonadal state commonly associated with various endocrinopathies [S2]

**Question:** Group III ovulation disorders result from what ovarian condition? [S2]  
**A)** Increased ovarian sensitivity to hormones  
**B)** Depleted ovarian function  
**C)** Excess follicle development

**Rationale:** Group III disorders are secondary to depleted ovarian function and result in hypergonadotropic hypogonadism [S2]
