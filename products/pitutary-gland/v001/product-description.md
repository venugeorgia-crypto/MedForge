**Educational Study Pack: Pituitary Gland – Fundamentals & Clinical Relevance**  

This study pack provides concise, evidence-based educational content on the anatomy, function, and clinical significance of the pituitary gland. Designed for medical students and healthcare learners, it covers core physiological principles and common clinical scenarios related to pituitary disorders.  

**What’s Included:**  
- Anatomical structure of the pituitary gland, including the anterior (adenohypophysis), posterior (neurohypophysis), and intermediate lobes [S2].  
- The role of the hypothalamus in regulating pituitary hormone secretion [S2].  
- Key endocrine functions of the pituitary, including hormone production affecting growth and other glands [S4].  
- Clinical presentation of pituitary tumors, including symptoms such as headaches, vision changes, nausea, and hormonal imbalances (e.g., Cushing’s syndrome, hyperthyroidism) [S4].  
- Evidence of local renin-angiotensin system activity in experimental pituitary tumors in rats [S5].  
- Case example illustrating neurological manifestations of prolactinoma, including galactorrhea and menstrual disruption [S6].  

**Target Audience:**  
Medical students, undergraduate health science learners, and professionals seeking foundational knowledge in endocrinology and neuroendocrinology.
